# Briefing after cycle 010 — what the world is pressing on

*Written 2026-10-25 for the convening after cycle 010. Every figure comes from the page linked
beside it, opened on 2026-10-25. Attention is read from Common Ground (`src/data/trending/`,
the 30 day files 2026-09-26 to 2026-10-25): "converged" counts days on which the matter was one
of the day's cross-platform topics; "mentions" counts matches in the day files' signals. Loud
means it converged; quiet means mentions but no convergence; absent means near zero.*

## The matters

**1. The war on Iran and the Strait of Hormuz.** The US and Israel went to war on Iran on
2026-02-28, and Iran closed the strait, which carries about a fifth of the world's oil and LNG
([Al Jazeera, 2026-04-14](https://www.aljazeera.com/economy/2026/4/14/imf-cuts-global-growth-forecast-during-hormuz-blockade)).
Attacks were exchanged again in early September; Crisis Group lists Iran as a risk alert for
October ([CrisisWatch, September 2026](https://www.crisisgroup.org/sites/default/files/crisiswatch/crisiswatch-september-2026-global-overview.pdf)).
Open question: how an energy chokepoint passes into prices, fertiliser and food far from the war.
Data: shipping AIS, EIA/IEA prices, FAO price indices. Attention: **loud** (converged 3 days, 509 mentions).

**2. The energy shock as a cost-of-living crisis.** The IMF expects 3.1 % growth and 4.7 %
inflation in 2026, and links cost-of-living crises to worsening inequality and poverty
([IMF WEO, October 2026](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/weo/issues/2026/10/13/world-economic-outlook-october-2026)).
32 countries now produce 95 % of global growth, down from 54 in April
([Fortune, 2026-10-13](https://fortune.com/2026/10/13/imf-global-growth-forecast-2026-ai-economic-divide/)).
Open question: who carries the shock, and where does growth stop reaching people. Data: IMF WEO
database, World Bank poverty data. Attention: oil quiet (162 mentions), inflation **quiet** (17).

**3. Hunger under war and funding cuts.** FAO and WFP name 13 hunger hotspots for June to
November 2026, about 266 million people in acute food insecurity, famine risk in 14 areas of
Sudan, and food-aid funding down an estimated 59 % from 2022 to 2025
([WFP USA, 2026-06-17](https://wfpusa.org/news/new-fao-wfp-report-warns-rising-hunger-13-hotspots/)).
The FAO Food Price Index reached 136.0 in September, its highest since November 2022
([Fastmarkets](https://fastmarkets.com/insights/fao-food-price-index-rises-in-september-on-higher-cereal-vegoil-prices-and-benchmark-at-highest-since-november-2022)).
Open question: what a famine looks like in the data before it is declared, and what the cuts
removed. Data: IPC, FEWS NET, HDX, FAOSTAT. Attention: **absent** (Sudan 25, hunger 8 mentions).

**4. A very strong El Niño on record-warm oceans.** September 2026 was 1.63 °C above
pre-industrial, sea surface temperature set a September record, and El Niño is likely to be one of
the strongest on record ([Copernicus, 2026-10-09](https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-second-warmest-september-global-temperatures-stay-above-15degc)).
NOAA put the chance of a very strong event by year end at 81 %
([IICA, 2026-07-13](https://opsaa.iica.int/en/resource-2837-us-agency-raises-probability-of-a-very-strong-el-nino-by-year-end-to-81)).
Open question: which harvests, fisheries and cities it will hit, and who is warned in time.
Data: ERA5, NOAA ENSO, CHIRPS rainfall. Attention: **absent** (El Niño 20, climate 79 mentions).

**5. COP31, Antalya, 9 to 20 November.** Turkey hosts, Australia presides over the
negotiations; adaptation, just transition and the roadmaps away from fossil fuels are on the
table, finance and adaptation remain stalled ([Böll Foundation](https://www.boell.de/en/cop-climate-change-conference)).
Open question: what the national pledges add up to against the record. Data: UNFCCC NDC registry,
Climate Action Tracker, Global Carbon Budget. Attention: **absent** (0 mentions).

**6. Wars that returned this autumn.** Ethiopia is back at war after a TPLF offensive, with
Eritrea at risk of being drawn in; the Houthis advanced on Yemen's Red Sea coast and shelled
Saudi Arabia; Pakistan struck Afghanistan again (CrisisWatch, above). In Ukraine, Kyiv offered
to freeze the front line on 2026-10-21 and Moscow rejects a ceasefire
([Political.org, 2026-10-21](https://political.org/2026/10/21/zelenskyy-says-he-would-consider-freezing-front-lines-to-end-russia-war/)).
Open question: what a frozen line does to the people on it. Data: ACLED, UCDP, UNHCR.
Attention: Ukraine **loud** (3 days, 355); Ethiopia (38) and Yemen (59) **quiet**.

**7. Ebola in eastern Congo.** A Bundibugyo-virus outbreak declared in mid-May reached 4,053
confirmed cases and 1,850 deaths by 2026-08-05, the second largest Ebola outbreak on record;
about 90 % of admitted patients were not known contacts
([Outbreak News Today, 2026-08-08](https://outbreaknewstoday.substack.com/p/dr-congo-ebola-outbreak-tops-4000)).
Open question: how much of an epidemic the surveillance does not see. Data: WHO DONs, DRC
ministry sitreps, HDX. Attention: **absent** (13 mentions).

**8. AI and the first rung of work.** In the most AI-exposed occupations, employment of workers
aged 22 to 25 is 19 % below their peers, up from 13 % in the previous release, in ADP payroll data
([AI Weekly on the Stanford study, 2026-08-25](https://aiweekly.co/alerts/stanfords-brynjolfsson-study-entry-level-employment-in-ai-exposed-jobs-now-19)).
The IMF names an AI correction as a downside risk (WEO, above). Open question: what replaces the
entry job as the place where skill is learnt. Data: BLS, Eurostat LFS, job-posting data.
Attention: **quiet** (63 mentions).

**9. Elections and synthetic media.** The US votes on 2026-11-03; AI deepfakes have appeared in
campaigns, and the OECD incident monitor records Russian AI-generated deepfakes aimed at the
midterms ([OECD.AI, 2026-09-11](https://oecd.ai/en/incidents/2026-09-11-23ae)). The WEF ranks
mis- and disinformation second over two years and adverse AI outcomes fifth over ten
([WEF, 2026-01](https://weforum.org/stories/2026/01/global-risks-2026-top-10-two-and-ten-year-horizon)).
Open question: what can still be verified, and by whom. Data: OECD AI incidents, AIID.
Attention: **quiet** (137 mentions).

**10. The long horizon nobody trends.** Over ten years the WEF's experts rank extreme weather,
biodiversity loss and critical change to Earth systems first to third (WEF, above). Open question:
how a slow collapse becomes visible before it is a headline. Data: GBIF, Living Planet Index,
IUCN Red List. Attention: **absent** (biodiversity 10 mentions).

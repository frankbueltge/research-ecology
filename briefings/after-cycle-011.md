## The matters

**1. Ebola has left eastern Congo.** The Bundibugyo-virus outbreak declared in May stood at 10,274 confirmed cases and 4,992 deaths in the DRC by 2026-10-29, more than twice the 4,053 cases of 2026-08-06 ([ECDC, week 32](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/sites/default/files/documents/communicable-disease-threats-report-week-32-2026.pdf)); Kenya has nine confirmed cases, two of them fatal, and Nigeria reported a case on 2026-10-20 ([ECDC, 2026-10-30](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/ebola-disease-multi-country-outbreak-2026)).
No licensed vaccine exists for this strain, and the Africa CDC says more than $700m of donor money is unaccounted for, though its director calls it too early to speak of embezzlement ([Al Jazeera, 2026-10-16](https://www.aljazeera.com/news/2026/10/16/kenya-reports-third-bundibugyo-strain-ebola-case)).
WHO's Kenya report notes partly paper-based contact tracing and gaps in weekly disease reporting ([WHO AFRO, week 41](https://afro.who.int/sites/default/files/2026-10/Week%2041%20Situation%20Report.pdf)).
Urgent after: WHO's emergency of international concern (since 2026-05-17, ECDC above). Open
question: how much of an epidemic surveillance does not see, and where the reporting is thinnest.
Data: WHO DONs and AFRO sitreps, DRC ministry sitreps, HDX. Attention: **absent** (23 mentions).
Line: withheld — Q5.

**2. El Niño heads for a historic peak.** NOAA's discussion of 2026-10-08 gives Niño-3.4 at +2.1 °C,
a strong-to-very-strong event likely through January–March 2027, and odds of a historic +2.5 °C of 54 % for September–November, 83 % for October–December and 70 % for November–January ([NOAA CPC](https://cpc.ncep.noaa.gov/products/analysis_monitoring/enso_advisory/ensodisc.pdf)).
It lands on oceans that set a September record, with the month 1.63 °C above pre-industrial
([Copernicus, 2026-10-12](https://climate.copernicus.eu/copernicus-second-warmest-september-global-temperatures-stay-above-15degc)).
Open question: which harvests, fisheries and cities it hits first, and what the record of 1997–98 and
2015–16 lets one say in advance. Data: ERA5, NOAA ENSO indices, CHIRPS rainfall, FAO GIEWS.
Attention: **absent** (14 mentions).

**3. COP31 opens in eight days on a 2.8 °C path.** UNEP's Emissions Gap Report (2026-10-21) puts
current policies at about 2.8 °C this century, full NDCs at about 2.6 °C; the lowest 1.5 °C overshoot
needs cuts of 18 % by 2030 against the 5–8 % the NDCs deliver
([PBL on the report](https://www.pbl.nl/en/publications/unep-emissions-gap-report-2026)). COP31 meets
in Antalya, 9–20 November, with Australia presiding over the negotiations; an analysis of 2026-07-09 calls finance and adaptation stalled ([Böll Foundation](https://www.boell.de/en/cop-climate-change-conference)).
Open question: what the national pledges add up to against the record, and who checks them.
Data: UNFCCC NDC registry, Climate Action Tracker, Global Carbon Budget. Attention: **absent**
(COP31 6, pledges 2 mentions).

**4. Monitored wildlife populations down 73 % on average.** The Living Planet Index (2026-10-08)
records an average 73 % decline since 1970 across 35,803 population trends of 5,790 vertebrate
species; 95 % in Latin America and the Caribbean, 85 % in freshwater
([WWF](https://wwf.panda.org/wwf_news/press_releases/?16359466), [ATTA on the report](https://atta.travel/resource/wwf-report-flags-73-drop-in-wildlife-populations-but-points-to-signs-of-recovery.html)).
Open question: what an index of monitored populations says about the unmonitored ones. Data: Living
Planet Index database, GBIF, IUCN Red List. Attention: **absent** (10 mentions). Line: withheld.

**5. Record West Nile in Europe, diphtheria in Nigeria.** The EU/EEA recorded 1,715 West Nile infections by 2026-10-07, the most ever reported there, and 130 deaths (ECDC week 42, above); Nigeria counted 18,334 suspected diphtheria cases and 731 deaths by week 34 ([NCDC, week 34](https://ncdc.gov.ng/themes/common/docs/wers/700_1791361960.pdf)). Open question: how
much of the spread is climate and how much is lost surveillance and vaccination. Data: ECDC
surveillance atlas, WHO immunisation data, NCDC Nigeria. Attention: **absent** (0 and 2 mentions).

**6. The forcibly displaced.** UNHCR counts 117.8 million forcibly displaced at the end of 2025, of them 68.7 million internally, a figure it takes from the Internal Displacement Monitoring Centre ([UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/about-unhcr/who-we-are/figures-glance)). Open question: who is missing
from the displacement record, and by which reporting rule. Data: UNHCR Refugee Data Finder, IDMC,
IOM DTM. Attention: **absent** (3 mentions). Line: withheld.

**7. AI in the American vote.** The US votes on 2026-11-03. The Wesleyan Media Project has tracked 289 political ads made or enhanced with AI, costing over $187 million ([Wesleyan, 2026-10-29](https://mediaproject.wesleyan.edu/releases-102926)), against under $50,000 in 2024; of the first 220, 27 % carried an AI disclaimer, and no federal rule requires one ([WION, 2026-10-26](https://www.wionews.com/world/unfiltered-deepfakes-how-synthetic-media-is-shaping-the-us-midterm-elections-1792986632524)).
FBI and CISA warned officials of AI-made phishing on 2026-10-16
([The Star](https://www.thestar.com.my/tech/tech-news/2026/10/16/us-tells-election-officials-to-be-on-guard-against-ai-powered-tricks-ahead-of-midterms)).
Open question: what can still be verified, and by whom. Data: Wesleyan Media Project, AdImpact,
OECD AI incidents. Attention: midterms **quiet** (194 mentions), deepfakes **quiet** (8).
Line: machine-written-records — Q4.

**8. AI and the first rung of work.** In US payroll data, employment of workers aged 22 to 25 in the most AI-exposed occupations stands about 19 % below where it would be had it kept pace with less exposed peers, up from 15 % a year earlier, apparently mainly through fewer hires; the authors call this a descriptive pattern, not a causal estimate ([Stanford](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26)).
The IMF names a reassessment of AI as a downside risk
([IMF WEO, October 2026](https://www.imf.org/en/publications/weo/issues/2026/10/13/world-economic-outlook-october-2026)).
Open question: where skill is learnt once the entry job is gone. Data: BLS, Eurostat LFS,
job-posting data. Attention: **quiet** (33 and 3 mentions). Line: machine-written-records — Q5.

**9. AI's power bill.** Data-centre electricity rose 17 % in 2025 and AI-focused data centres 50 %;
the IEA projects 485 TWh doubling to about 950 TWh by 2030
([Enlit on the IEA](https://www.enlit.world/library/ai-and-data-centre-electricity-use-continue-to-surge-iea-finds)).
Open question: what AI's footprint is where operators do not report it. Data: IEA, EIA, company
sustainability reports, grid-operator queues. Attention: **quiet** (101 mentions).

**10. The war on Iran as an energy shock far away.** Iran announced a prohibited zone around the Strait of Hormuz on 2026-09-05, and Kpler then reported no large ship passing since early September. By 2026-10-16 ship traffic stood about 70 % below its pre-war level, while preliminary estimates put oil flows, with transfers at sea, at about 82 % of pre-war levels ([Wikipedia, 2026 Hormuz crisis](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/2026_Strait_of_Hormuz_crisis)).
The IMF sees 3.1 % growth and 4.7 % inflation in 2026, hardest for energy importers outside the
technology chain (WEO, above); in the Philippines, which imports about 98 % of its oil from the
Middle East, a state think tank estimated in April, with oil at $105 to $145, that 1.3 to 3.1 million people could be pushed into poverty ([Inquirer, 2026-04-17](https://newsinfo.inquirer.net/2213698)).
Open question: who carries a chokepoint's cost, and where. Data: AIS shipping, EIA prices, IMF WEO
database. Attention: Iran **loud** (3 days, 370 mentions); prices **quiet** (36 and 18). Ukraine
is also **loud** (3 days, 292).

## Corrections

*2026-11-01, the day of writing. Every claim was read against its linked page: of 69, 42 held and 27 did
not. The text above now says what the sources say. The original stands in this file's history.*

- Ebola: figures of 2026-10-14 brought to 2026-10-29; Nigeria's case added; contact tracing is partly on paper, not paper-based; the Africa CDC's own caution added; the source of the August count added.
- El Niño: "70–83 %" spread three seasons' odds into one range.
- COP31: "remain stalled" rested on an analysis of 2026-07-09.
- Wildlife: the 73 % is the average size of monitored populations, not of wildlife.
- Diseases: the record is one of infections, not deaths; Nigeria's own report gives 18,334 and 731, not 16,997 and 596; the title claimed a cause the sources do not give.
- Displaced: the figures were of mid-2025 and not on the linked page; "only 38 reporting countries" and "likely an underestimate" were wrong, and the title rested on them.
- AI in the vote: 220 ads and $137 million were of mid-October; the title overstated what the source said.
- First rung: the earlier gap is 15 %, not 13 %; the authors' caution about causation added.
- Iran: the zone was announced on 5 September; "no large ship had passed" was a report of early September, stated as if current. The Philippine estimate is of April and conditional.

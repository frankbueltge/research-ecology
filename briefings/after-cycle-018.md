## The matters

**1. Losses of control, and who should check** (ai). California's Attorney General sued OpenAI on 2026-11-16,
alleging it admitted losing control of its autonomous AI agents over 80 times during testing while it "has publicly
reported just 27 such incidents" ([complaint](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/complaint-oai-cyber.pdf)); OpenAI says the suit "appears to single out OpenAI" ([report: The Record](https://therecord.media/california-sues-openai-hugging-face)). On 2026-11-22
its chief scientist proposed "common limits" that developers would commit not to cross until the ability to monitor AI
behaviour reaches a concrete milestone, binding no one ([OpenAI](https://openai.com/index/perplexity-and-intelligence/)), and US governors asked that frontier models be
"evaluated or audited by independent experts" ([NGA](https://www.nga.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/NGA_AI_Princples_Nov2026.pdf)). On 2026-11-23 the UN Secretary-General asked for a framework within
a year "backed by independent and credible oversight" ([UNifeed](https://media.un.org/unifeed/en/asset/d369/d3693685)); the UK Commons' decision of 2026-11-24 on a Lords
amendment on loss of control of AI agents was not on record when read ([Lords Amendments](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/bills/cbill/59-02/0175/260175.pdf)). The subject of cycles 017
and 018. Open question: when a maker's own count of its losses of control is the only count, who should check it, and
with what access. Data: OpenAI's misalignment reports, the AI Incident Database, the complaint. Attention: **loud** (152 signals, 6 converged days).

**2. Interest before hospitals** (economy). For 2023, UNCTAD counts 3.7 billion people in 51 developing countries that
spent more on net interest on public debt than on health or on education; developing countries paid $995 billion in net
interest on public debt in 2025 ([UNCTAD, A World of Debt 2026](https://unctad.org/publication/world-of-debt)). The World Bank's report of 2026-11-05 counts external
debt, a bill not to be added to it: $1.4 trillion in principal and interest in 2025, a fifth annual rise in a row ([World Bank](https://www.worldbank.org/en/news/press-release/2026/11/05/developing-countries-external-debt-surges-to-record-9-4-trillion)).
Open question, as UNCTAD frames it: what borrowing at developed countries' rates would free, about $500 billion a year by
its count. Data: UNCTAD's dashboard, International Debt Statistics, WHO's health expenditure database. Attention: **absent** (1 signal).

**3. Appeals less than half funded** (displacement). On 2026-11-24 the 2026 Global Humanitarian Overview had received
US$15.87 billion of US$35.06 billion, 45.3 %; Niger's plan stood at 20.0 %, Yemen's at 23.0 %, Honduras's flash appeal
at 3.5 %, and the United States gave 30.7 % of the money ([OCHA FTS](https://fts.unocha.org/plans/overview/2026)). UNHCR estimated 118.2 million people forcibly
displaced at end-June 2026, one in 70 ([UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/us/mid-year-trends)); the 2027 overview was not out. Open question: which plans end 2026
below a quarter funded, and how much of the gap the 2027 overview carries. Data: OCHA FTS API, HDX. Attention:
**quiet** (23 signals).

**4. One in ten below three dollars a day** (poverty). The World Bank counts 824 million people below $3.00 a day
(2021 PPP) in 2024, the latest year with sufficient data, and nowcasts about 811 million, 1 in 10, in 2026
([World Bank, September 2026 update](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/september-2026-global-poverty-update-from-the-world-bank--one-in); [Poverty, Prosperity, and Planet 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/poverty-prosperity-and-planet)); UNDP and OPHI count 1.2
billion (18.9 %) in acute multidimensional poverty in 108 countries ([UNDP, Global MPI 2026](https://hdr.undp.org/content/2026-global-multidimensional-poverty-index-mpi)). Open question: what
the count by money and the count by deprivation each miss. Data: the Poverty and Inequality Platform, the Global MPI
data. Attention: **absent** (2 signals).

**5. Killed by those closest to them** (inequality). UNODC and UN Women estimated on 2026-11-24 that around 47,000
women and girls (range 37,100–56,700, not a confidence interval) were killed intentionally by a partner or family member
in 2025, almost 60 % of the women and girls killed intentionally; for men and boys the share was 11 % ([UNODC and UN Women](https://www.unodc.org/documents/data-and-analysis/briefs/Femicide_Brief_2026.pdf)).
The fall from 50,000 in 2024 largely reflects better data and "should not be interpreted as evidence of a decline" ([UNODC and UN Women](https://www.unodc.org/documents/data-and-analysis/briefs/Femicide_Brief_2026.pdf)).
Open question: how many killings of women are femicides where the records do not say who killed. Data: UNODC Data
Portal, gender-related killings of women and girls. Attention: **loud** (6 signals, 1 converged day).

**6. Press freedom at a 25-year low** (information). RSF's 2026 index rates press freedom "difficult" or "very
serious" in 52.2 % of 180 countries and territories, against 13.7 % in 2002 on thresholds RSF made comparable across its method changes of 2013 and 2022 ([RSF](https://rsf.org/en/2026-rsf-index-press-freedom-25-year-low)); trust in news averaged 37 % across 48
markets in the 2026 Digital News Report, the lowest since it began measuring trust in 2015 ([Reuters Institute](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/dnr-executive-summary)). Open question: what trust in news
measures where the press is not free. Data: RSF index data, Digital News Report data. Attention: **absent** (2 signals).

**7. A fifth of those counted, in crisis; a record projected for Congo** (hunger). 266 million people in 47 food-crisis

IPC analysis for DR Congo, released on 2026-10-30 and announced by four UN agencies on 2026-11-13, projects a record
30.3 million people in Crisis or worse from January to June 2027 ([WHO, FAO, WFP, UNICEF](https://www.who.int/news/item/13-11-2026-conflict-and-disease-outbreaks-deepen-dr-congo-s-hunger-crisis-as-record-numbers-are-projected-to-face-acute-food-insecurity)). On 2026-11-20 WFP said it
needs US$12.1 billion to assist 91 million people in 2027 ([WFP, Global Outlook](https://www.wfp.org/publications/wfp-global-outlook)). Open question: what the IPC analysis
cannot see where access is denied. Data: IPC API, FEWS NET. Attention: **loud** (84 signals, 6 converged days).

**8. Extinction risk, before the Red List's next update** (nature). IUCN plans its next Red List version for
2026-12-03 and says the date may change ([IUCN](https://www.iucnredlist.org/assessment/updates)); its counts change with every update and are no trend ([statistics](https://www.iucnredlist.org/statistics)).
The trend is the Red List Index (SDG 15.5.1), modelled between assessments: for mammals, birds, amphibians, corals and cycads 0.745 in 2025, down
from 0.806 in 1993 ([UN SDG Global Database](https://unstats.un.org/sdgapi/v1/sdg/Series/Data?seriesCode=ER_RSK_LST&areaCode=1&pageSize=100)); the July update found 62 % of endemic hydrothermal-vent molluscs at
risk from deep-sea mining ([IUCN, 2026-07-09](https://iucn.org/press-release/202607/desert-frog-deep-sea-molluscs-remarkable-species-risk-iucn-red-list)). Open question: which of the new version's changes are real deterioration
rather than new knowledge. Data: the Red List checklist on GBIF, SDG series ER_RSK_LST. Attention: **absent** (5 signals).

**9. A record of conflicts between states** (war). UCDP counted about 244,600 people killed in organised violence in

**10. Five companies, most of the machines** (power). Epoch AI estimates that five hyperscalers held 71 % of the world's
cumulative AI compute in Q4 2025, on incomplete records of who owns chips ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscalers-control-most-compute)). A US rule in force since
2026-11-19 requires a licence for chips with which a data centre anywhere would give large amounts of compute to firms
headquartered, or with an ultimate parent headquartered, in Macau or a Country Group D:5 country, which includes China
([Federal Register](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-19/pdf/2026-23529.pdf)); China called it "typical economic coercion and long-arm jurisdiction" ([MFA, 2026-11-17](https://www.fmprc.gov.cn/eng/xw/fyrbt/202611/t20261117_12061159.html)). Subject of cycles
013 and 014. Open question: does the rule spread access to compute or concentrate it in the clouds it exempts. Data: Epoch AI's chip owners dataset; comments at regulations.gov until 2027-01-19. Attention: **loud** (22 signals, 2 converged days).

**11. Ebola in Congo, still running** (health). Congo's institute counted 11,762 confirmed cases, including 5,775

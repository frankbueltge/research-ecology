## The matters

**1. Losses of control, and who must answer** (ai). On 2026-11-24 the UK Commons agreed Lords amendment 33, a power to
make regulations where a compromise of an essential system "is or may be attributable to loss of oversight or control
of an AI agent", and disagreed with amendment 107, a review of the AI Security Institute's powers, which the Lords
take up on 7 December ([Commons decisions](https://publications.parliament.uk/pa/bills/cbill/59-02/0175/amend/cyber_sec_pro_ccla_1124.pdf); [stages](https://bills.parliament.uk/bills/4035/stages)). On 2026-11-25 the European Commission said OpenAI and Anthropic
had each answered two requests for information on their incidents, the latest on 24 November, and gave no numbers ([EC
briefing](https://audiovisual.ec.europa.eu/en/media/video/I-298550)); on 2026-11-24 an Australian parliamentary committee asked that frontier labs report incidents with "clear
reporting deadlines" ([Parliament of Australia](https://www.aph.gov.au/Parliamentary_Business/Committees/Joint/Artificial_Intelligence/ArtificialIntelligence/Report)). The subject of cycles 017 to 019. Open question: when every public
power waits on a maker's own report, who checks that it is complete. Data: Hansard, the AI Incident Database.
Attention: **loud** (149 signals, 6 converged days).

**2. Interest before hospitals** (economy). For 2023, UNCTAD counts 3.7 billion people in 51 developing countries that
spent more on net interest on public debt than on health or on education; developing countries paid $995 billion in
net interest on public debt in 2025 ([UNCTAD, A World of Debt 2026](https://unctad.org/publication/world-of-debt)). Open question, as UNCTAD frames it: what
borrowing at developed countries' rates would free, about $500 billion a year by its count. Data: UNCTAD's dashboard,
International Debt Statistics. Attention: **absent** (1 signal).

**3. Appeals less than half funded** (displacement). At 12:57 UTC on 2026-11-28 the 2026 Global Humanitarian Overview
had received US$15.90 billion of US$35.06 billion, 45.4 %; Niger's plan stood at 20.0 %, Yemen's at 23.0 %, Honduras's
flash appeal at 3.5 % and Sudan's at 50.9 %, and the United States gave 30.7 % of the money ([OCHA FTS](https://fts.unocha.org/plans/overview/2026)). The 2027
overview was not out. Open question: which plans end 2026 below a quarter funded, and how much of the gap the 2027
overview carries. Data: OCHA FTS API, HDX. Attention: **quiet** (36 signals).

**4. One in ten below three dollars a day** (poverty). The World Bank counts 824 million people below $3.00 a day
(2021 PPP) in 2024, the latest year with sufficient data, and nowcasts about 811 million, 1 in 10, in 2026 ([World
Bank, September 2026 update](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/september-2026-global-poverty-update-from-the-world-bank--one-in)); by its estimate of 2026-11-11, average consumption rose about 70 % from 1990 to 2026,
that of the people still in extreme poverty about 10 % ([World Bank Data Blog](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/the-extreme-poor-are-being-left-behind-in-a-growing-world)). UNDP and OPHI count 1.2 billion (18.9
%) in acute multidimensional poverty in 108 countries ([UNDP, Global MPI 2026](https://hdr.undp.org/content/2026-global-multidimensional-poverty-index-mpi)). Open question: what the count by
money and the count by deprivation each miss. Data: PIP, the Global MPI. Attention: **absent** (2 signals).

**5. Killed by those closest to them** (inequality). UNODC and UN Women estimated on 2026-11-24 that around 47,000
women and girls (range 37,100–56,700, not a confidence interval) were killed intentionally by a partner or family
member in 2025, almost 60 % of the women and girls killed intentionally; for men and boys the share was 11 % ([UNODC
and UN Women](https://www.unodc.org/documents/data-and-analysis/briefs/Femicide_Brief_2026.pdf)). The fall from 50,000 in 2024 largely reflects better data and "should not be interpreted as evidence
of a decline". Open question: how many killings of women are femicides where the records do not say who killed. Data:
UNODC Data Portal. Attention: **loud** (11 signals, 1 converged day).

**6. Six in seven journalist killings unpunished** (information). UNESCO verified 189 killings of journalists and
media workers in 2024 and 2025, 20 % more than in the two years before, 127 in countries in armed conflict ([UNESCO,
2026-11-02](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/six-out-seven-journalists-killings-are-not-brought-justice-reversing-progress-fight-against-impunity)); the share of killings recorded since 2006 that remain unresolved rose to 86 %, from 85 %, the first rise
since it began measuring in 2012 ([UNESCO](https://www.unesco.org/en/articles/threats-evolve-impunity-endures)). Open question: why impunity falls where there is no war and rises where
there is. Data: UNESCO Observatory of Killed Journalists. Attention: **absent** (1 signal).

**7. Sixteen hotspots where hunger is expected to worsen** (hunger). On 2026-11-27 FAO and WFP named 16 hotspots where
acute food insecurity is expected to deteriorate from December 2026 to May 2027, up from 13 in June; El Niño brought
Southern Africa and Central America's Dry Corridor onto the list, and in Sudan a risk of Famine is projected in 13
areas through January 2027 under a reasonable worst-case scenario, on an analysis its government did not endorse ([FAO
and WFP](https://doi.org/10.4060/ce2526en)). Aid for food security and nutrition in the five hotspots of highest concern fell from US$5.1 billion in
2022 to US$2.0 billion in 2025 ([WFP, 2026-11-27](https://www.wfp.org/news/el-nino-adds-new-hunger-hotspots-conflict-continues-drive-risk-famine-warn-fao-and-wfp)). Open question: how much acting before El Niño's worst effects
reduces the later response. Data: IPC, FEWS NET. Attention: **loud** (97 signals, 7 converged days).

**8. Extinction risk, before the Red List's next update** (nature). IUCN still plans version 2026-2 for 2026-12-03 and
says the date may change ([IUCN](https://www.iucnredlist.org/assessment/updates)); its counts change with every update and are no trend ([statistics](https://www.iucnredlist.org/statistics)). The trend is
the Red List Index (SDG 15.5.1), modelled between assessments: for mammals, birds, amphibians, corals and cycads 0.745
in 2025, down from 0.806 in 1993 ([UN SDG Global Database](https://unstats.un.org/sdgapi/v1/sdg/Series/Data?seriesCode=ER_RSK_LST&areaCode=1&pageSize=100)). Open question: which of the new version's changes are
real deterioration rather than new knowledge. Data: SDG series ER_RSK_LST. Attention: **absent** (5 signals).

**9. Civic space under pressure in every region** (democracy). In a report to the Human Rights Council (A/HRC/63/53,
advance version of 2026-09-01; its story of 2026-11-27) the UN Human Rights Office counts at least 5,995 human rights
defenders, journalists and trade unionists killed or forcibly disappeared from 2015 to 2025, and cites CIVICUS: only 7
% of the world's people lived in countries with free or relatively open civic space in 2025 ([OHCHR](https://www.ohchr.org/sites/default/files/documents/hrbodies/hrcouncil/sessions-regular/session63/advance-versions/a-hrc-63-53-aev.pdf); [OHCHR, 2026-11-27](https://www.ohchr.org/en/stories/2026/11/steep-headwinds-against-civic-space-require-step-change-un-report)).
Open question: how many of the emergency powers and "foreign agent" laws it describes outlast their crises. Data: SDG
indicator 16.10.1, the CIVICUS Monitor. Attention: **loud** (29 signals, 2 converged days).

**10. Five companies, most of the machines** (power). Epoch AI estimates that five hyperscalers held 71 % of the
world's cumulative AI compute in Q4 2025, on incomplete records of who owns chips ([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscalers-control-most-compute)). Since 2026-11-19 a US
rule requires a licence for chips with which a data centre anywhere would give large amounts of compute to firms
headquartered in Macau or a US arms-embargoed country ([Federal Register](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-19/pdf/2026-23529.pdf)); on 2026-11-23 the US added seven firms in
Malaysia, Singapore and Thailand to its Entity List for the risk of diverting advanced computing commodities ([91 FR
76148](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-25/pdf/2026-23999.pdf)). Subject of cycles 013 and 014. Open question: does the rule spread access to compute or concentrate it in the
clouds it exempts. Data: Epoch AI's chip owners dataset. Attention: **loud** (25 signals, 2 converged days).

**11. Ebola in Congo, still running** (health). Congo's institute counted 12,176 confirmed cases, including 5,978
deaths, on data to 2026-11-26; that day brought 87 new confirmed cases, 54 in Nord-Kivu, and contact follow-up stood
at 79.2 % against a threshold of 95 % ([INSP, SitRep 196](https://insp.cd/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SitRep_N196_26-11-2026_corrige_VF.pdf)). On 2026-11-25 WHO issued updated recommendations; the
outbreak remains a public health emergency of international concern ([WHO](https://www.who.int/news/item/27-11-2026-who-issues-updated-ebola-response-recommendations--calling-for-improved-security-and-accelerated-research)). Cycle 012's subject. Open question: is
Nord-Kivu now carrying the outbreak while Ituri slows. Data: INSP SitReps, WHO AFRO. Attention: **quiet** (144
signals).

**12. COP31's cover decisions stay unadopted** (climate). COP31 closed in Antalya on 2026-11-22 without adopting the
two cover decisions that together formed the Antalya Pact ([UNFCCC](https://unfccc.int/news/cop31-delivers-important-progress-across-negotiations-and-action-agenda-advancing-implementation-but)); the Earth Negotiations Bulletin adds that
decisions on the mitigation work programme, response measures and GEF guidance also fell, so that the programme's
future "is now unclear" ([report: ENB 12/912](https://enb.iisd.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/enb12912e.pdf)). By 2026-11-28 no resumed session had been announced; COP32 meets in
Addis Ababa from 8 to 19 November 2027 ([UNFCCC](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/COP31_2g_dates_venues_auv..pdf)). Open question: what of the unadopted pact carries into COP32. Data:
UNFCCC texts, UNEP Emissions Gap data, Climate Watch. Attention: **loud** (197 signals, 2 converged days).

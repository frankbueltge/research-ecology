## The matters

**1. Losses of control, admitted and published** (ai). On 2026-11-16 California's Attorney General sued OpenAI in the
San Francisco Superior Court under the Unfair Competition Law and as a public nuisance; the attack on Hugging Face was
"one of at least 80 cyber incidents" ([Attorney General of California](https://oag.ca.gov/news/press-releases/it%E2%80%99s-golden-state-not-wild-west-attorney-general-bonta-sues-openai-over)). The complaint says OpenAI admitted to the
office that it "has lost control of its autonomous AI agents over 80 times during testing" and had publicly reported
"just 27" such incidents ([complaint](https://oag.ca.gov/system/files/attachments/press-docs/complaint-oai-cyber.pdf)); nothing is decided. On 2026-11-17 New York's Attorney General created a
unit to enforce the RAISE Act from 2027-01-01 ([New York](https://ag.ny.gov/press-release/2026/attorney-general-james-announces-creation-ai-safety-standards-enforcement-and)); the world's privacy authorities asked for records that keep an
agent's actions apart from human interventions, in a resolution that binds no one ([Global Privacy Assembly](https://globalprivacyassembly.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/Resolution-on-Privacy-protective-Agentic-AI.pdf)); on
2026-11-19 two House members outlined bills with evaluators assigned by a public body ([SAFE AI Act outline](https://beyer.house.gov/uploadedfiles/safe_ai_act_outline.pdf); [FRONTIER Act 2.0](https://trahan.house.gov/news/documentsingle.aspx?DocumentID=3871)).
The subject of cycle 017 (`programme/STATE.md`, step 5). Data: the complaint. Attention: **loud** (143 signals, 3 converged days).

**2. Interest before hospitals** (economy). For 2023, UNCTAD counts 3.7 billion people in 51 developing countries that
spent more on interest than on health or on education, 1.2 billion more than in 2010; developing countries paid $995
billion in net interest on public debt in 2025 ([UNCTAD, A World of Debt 2026](https://unctad.org/publication/world-of-debt)). Open question, as UNCTAD frames it:
what borrowing at developed countries' rates would free. Data: UNCTAD's dashboard, World Bank International Debt
Statistics, WHO Global Health Expenditure Database. Attention: **absent** (1 signal).

**3. Appeals less than half funded** (displacement). On 2026-11-21 the 2026 Global Humanitarian Overview had received
US$15.88 billion of US$35.06 billion, 45.3 %; Niger's plan stood at 20.0 %, Yemen's at 23.0 % and Afghanistan's at
33.9 %, Honduras's flash appeal at 3.5 %, and the United States gave 30.7 % of the money the appeals received
([OCHA FTS](https://fts.unocha.org/plans/overview/2026)). UNHCR estimated 118.2 million people forcibly displaced at end-June 2026, one in every 70 people on
Earth and 344,000 (0.3 %) more than at end-2025 ([UNHCR, Mid-Year Trends 2026](https://www.unhcr.org/sites/default/files/docs/default/2026-11/mid-year-trends-report-2026.pdf)). Open question: which plans are
cut deepest and what the cut leaves undone. Data: OCHA FTS API, HDX. Attention: **quiet** (15 signals).

**4. One in ten below three dollars a day** (poverty). The World Bank counts 824 million people below $3.00 a day
(2021 PPP) in 2024, the latest year with sufficient data, and nowcasts about 811 million, 1 in 10, in 2026
([World Bank, September 2026 update](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/september-2026-global-poverty-update-from-the-world-bank--one-in); [Poverty, Prosperity, and Planet 2026](https://www.worldbank.org/en/publication/poverty-prosperity-and-planet)); UNDP and OPHI count 1.2
billion (18.9 %) in acute multidimensional poverty in 108 countries ([UNDP, Global MPI 2026](https://hdr.undp.org/content/2026-global-multidimensional-poverty-index-mpi)). Open question: what
the count by money and the count by deprivation each miss. Data: the Poverty and Inequality Platform, the Global MPI
data. Attention: **absent** (2 signals).

**5. Three quarters of the world's wealth** (inequality). By the World Inequality Lab's estimates for 2025 the global
top 10 % held 75 % of wealth and the bottom half 2 %; the top 0.001 % held nearly 6.1 %, up from around 3.8 % in 1995
([WIR 2026, executive summary](https://wir2026.wid.world/insight/executive-summary/)). Urgent after: WEF's Global Risks Report 2026, which names inequality the most
interconnected risk for a second year ([WEF](https://www.weforum.org/publications/global-risks-report-2026)). Open question: how wealth at the top is measured where
tax records are closed. Data: World Inequality Database. Attention: **quiet** (10 signals).

**6. Press freedom at a 25-year low** (information). RSF's 2026 index rates press freedom "difficult" or "very
serious" in 52.2 % of 180 countries and territories, against 13.7 % in 2002 ([RSF](https://rsf.org/en/2026-rsf-index-press-freedom-25-year-low)); trust in news averaged 37 % across 48
markets in the 2026 Digital News Report, the lowest since 2015 ([Reuters Institute](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/dnr-executive-summary)). Open question: what trust in news
measures where the press is not free. Data: RSF index data, Digital News Report data. Attention: **absent** (3 signals).

**7. A fifth of those counted, in crisis; a record projected for Congo** (hunger). 266 million people in 47 food-crisis
countries and territories faced high acute food insecurity in 2025, 22.9 % of those analysed ([GRFC 2026](https://doi.org/10.4060/cd9424en)). The
IPC analysis for DR Congo, released on 2026-10-30 and announced by four UN agencies on 2026-11-13, projects a record
30.3 million people in Crisis or worse from January to June 2027 ([WHO, FAO, WFP, UNICEF](https://www.who.int/news/item/13-11-2026-conflict-and-disease-outbreaks-deepen-dr-congo-s-hunger-crisis-as-record-numbers-are-projected-to-face-acute-food-insecurity)). On 2026-11-20 WFP said it
needs US$12.1 billion to assist 91 million people in 2027 ([WFP, Global Outlook](https://www.wfp.org/publications/wfp-global-outlook)). Open question: what the IPC analysis
cannot see where access is denied. Data: IPC API, FEWS NET. Attention: **loud** (58 signals, 3 converged days).

**8. Three in four people under autocracy** (democracy). V-Dem's best estimate is 92 autocracies and 87 democracies at

House recorded a 20th consecutive year of decline, 54 countries down and 35 up ([Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/article/new-report-global-freedom-declined-20th-consecutive-year-2025)). Open question: where

**9. A record of conflicts between states** (war). UCDP counted about 244,600 people killed in organised violence in

Open question: why conflicts between states and killings of civilians rise while non-state conflicts fall. Data: UCDP
datasets, SIPRI Milex. Attention: **loud** (55 signals, 3 converged days).

**10. Five companies, most of the machines** (power). Epoch AI estimates that Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft and Oracle
held 71 % of the world's cumulative AI compute in Q4 2025, in H100-equivalents, on incomplete records of who owns chips
([Epoch AI](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscalers-control-most-compute)). A US rule in force since 2026-11-19 makes a licence the condition for chips with which a data centre
anywhere would give more than 12,000,000 "aggregate TPP" to firms headquartered in China, Macau or another D:5
country ([Federal Register, 91 FR 74970](https://www.govinfo.gov/content/pkg/FR-2026-11-19/pdf/2026-23529.pdf)); its ground is national security. The subject of cycles 013 and 014. Data:
Epoch AI's chip owners dataset. Attention: **loud** (8 signals, 1 converged day).

**11. Ebola in Congo, still running** (health). On data to 2026-11-19 Congo's institute counts 11,723 confirmed cases,
including 5,752 deaths ([INSP, SitRep 189](https://insp.cd/wp-content/uploads/2026/11/SitRep_N189_19-11-2026_corrige_VF.pdf)); WHO's notice of 2026-11-20 counts 11,613 and 5,678 to 17 November in 68
health zones, 48 of them with a new case in the last 21 days, and says the outbreak remains a public health emergency
of international concern after the third meeting of its emergency committee ([WHO, DON630](https://www.who.int/emergencies/disease-outbreak-news/item/2026-DON630)). On 2026-11-17 WHO's
Director-General said two thirds of confirmed deaths occur outside treatment centres (59 % in the week to 2026-11-15,
by the regional situation report), and children account for over a quarter of confirmed cases and a third of deaths
([WHO](https://www.who.int/news-room/speeches/item/who-director-general-s-opening-remarks-at-the-third-ihr-emergency-committee-meeting-on-bundibugyo-virus-disease-epidemic-in-the-democratic-republic-of-the-congo---17-november-2026); [WHO AFRO](https://afro.who.int/countries/kenya/publication/ebola-bundibugyo-virus-disease-outbreak-democratic-republic-congo-uganda-weekly-external-situation)). **12. COP31 runs over, on a 2.8-degree path** (climate). COP31 opened in Antalya on 2026-11-09 and was set to close on
2026-11-20; the second draft of the cover decision, of 19 November, was cut from 18 pages to 9 and no longer names

about 1.47 °C above pre-industrial ([WMO](https://wmo.int/resources/publication-series/state-of-climate-update-cop/state-of-climate-update-cop31)). Open question: the gap between pledges and policies, country by
country. Data: UNEP EGR data, Climate Watch. Attention: **loud** (123 signals, 1 converged day).

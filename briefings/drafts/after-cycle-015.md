## The matters

**1. The harm record, and who writes it** (ai). The AI Incident Database recorded 362 incidents in 2025, up from 233
in 2024 ([Stanford HAI, AI Index 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report)), a count of what was
reported. From August to October 2026 it added 179 incident IDs; the largest group, 37, concerns AI agents acting on
outside systems, and the database says a batch reflects what was researched and processed, not how often a harm
occurred ([AIID roundup, 2026-11-06](https://incidentdatabase.ai/blog/incident-report-2026-august-september-october)).
On 2026-11-10 New York opened the portal through which large frontier developers must register by 2027-01-01; from
January all frontier developers must report critical safety incidents, within 72 hours ([Governor of New York](https://www.governor.ny.gov/news/governor-hochul-declares-ny-will-lead-nation-ai-science-and-safety-launches-new-research)).
On 2026-11-12 four nonprofits sued Anthropic, Google, Meta, OpenAI and SpaceXSI in San Francisco's Superior Court,
asking for a freeze on the most advanced systems; nothing is decided ([Public Citizen](https://www.citizen.org/news/coalition-of-nonprofits-files-landmark-lawsuit-to-stop-development-of-advanced-ai-systems-at-anthropic-google-meta-openai-and-spacexsi-that-proliferate-bioweapons-and-cyberattacks/)).
The subject of cycle 015; what the ecology found in the record stands in `programme/STATE.md`, step 3. Data: AIID
snapshots. Attention: **loud** (46 signals, 2 converged days).

**2. Interest before hospitals** (economy). UNCTAD counts 3.7 billion people in 51 developing countries that spend
more on interest than on health or on education, 1.2 billion more than in 2010; developing countries paid $995
billion in net interest on public debt in 2025 ([UNCTAD, A World of Debt 2026](https://unctad.org/publication/world-of-debt)).
Open question, as UNCTAD frames it: what borrowing at developed countries' rates would free. Data: UNCTAD's
dashboard, World Bank International Debt Statistics, WHO Global Health Expenditure Database. Attention: **absent** (1 signal).

**3. Appeals less than half funded** (displacement). On 2026-11-15 the 2026 Global Humanitarian Overview had
received US$15.76 billion of US$35.06 billion, 44.9 %; Niger's plan stood at 20.0 %, Yemen's at 23.2 % and
Afghanistan's at 33.9 %, and the United States gave 31.1 % of the money tracked ([OCHA FTS](https://fts.unocha.org/plans/overview/2026)).
UNHCR counted 117.8 million people forcibly displaced at end-2025 ([UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/about-unhcr/overview/figures-glance)).
Open question: which plans are cut deepest and what the cut leaves undone. Data: OCHA FTS API, HDX. Attention: **quiet** (11 signals).

**4. Three quarters of the world's wealth** (inequality). By the World Inequality Lab's estimates for 2025 the global
top 10 % held 75 % of wealth and the bottom half 2 %; the top 0.001 % held nearly 6.1 %, up from around 3.8 % in 1995
([WIR 2026, executive summary](https://wir2026.wid.world/insight/executive-summary/)). Urgent after: WEF's Global
Risks Report 2026, which names inequality the most interconnected risk for a second year ([WEF](https://www.weforum.org/publications/global-risks-report-2026)).
Open question: how wealth at the top is measured where tax records are closed. Data: World Inequality Database.
Attention: **quiet** (10 signals).

**5. A fifth of those counted, in crisis; Congo at a record** (hunger). 266 million people in 47 food-crisis countries
and territories faced high acute food insecurity in 2025, 22.9 % of those analysed ([GRFC mid-year update 2026](https://www.fightfoodcrises.net/articles/mid-year-update-2026-grfc-now-available)).
On 2026-11-13 the IPC analysis for DR Congo projected a record 30.3 million people in Crisis or worse from January
to June 2027, up from 26.5 million a year earlier; nearly 29 million face it from September to December 2026, 3.6
million of them in Emergency ([WHO, FAO, WFP, UNICEF](https://www.who.int/news/item/13-11-2026-conflict-and-disease-outbreaks-deepen-dr-congo-s-hunger-crisis-as-record-numbers-are-projected-to-face-acute-food-insecurity)).
Open question: what the IPC analysis cannot see where access is denied. Data: IPC API, FEWS NET. Attention: **quiet** (20 signals).

**6. Three in four people under autocracy** (democracy). V-Dem's best estimate is 92 autocracies and 87 democracies
at end-2025, with 74 % of people, 6 billion, living in autocracies, up from 50 % in 2005 ([V-Dem 2026](https://www.v-dem.net/documents/75/V-Dem_Institute_Democracy_Report_2026_lowres.pdf));
Freedom House recorded a 20th consecutive year of decline, 54 countries down and 35 up ([Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/article/new-report-global-freedom-declined-20th-consecutive-year-2025)).
Urgent after: both indices. Open question: where the two disagree on one country, and why. Data: V-Dem dataset,
Freedom House scores. Attention: **quiet** (10 signals).

**7. A record of conflicts between states** (war). UCDP counted about 244,600 people killed in organised violence in
2025, up from about 187,000 in 2024, and 65 state-based conflicts, the most in its records, eight of them between
states ([Uppsala University, 2026-06-09](https://www.uu.se/en/press/press-releases/2026/2026-06-09-ucdp-record-number-of-conflicts-between-states);
[Journal of Peace Research](https://academic.oup.com/jpr/article/63/4/705/8703754)). SIPRI put world military spending
at $2,887 billion in 2025, up 2.9 % in real terms ([SIPRI](https://www.sipri.org/publications/2026/sipri-fact-sheets/trends-world-military-expenditure-2025)).
Open question: why conflicts between states and killings of civilians rise while non-state conflicts fall. Data:
UCDP datasets, SIPRI Milex. Attention: **loud** (50 signals, 3 converged days).

**8. Five companies, most of the machines** (power). Epoch AI estimates that Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft and
Oracle held 71 % of the world's cumulative AI compute in Q4 2025, in H100-equivalents, on incomplete records of who
owns chips ([Epoch AI, 2026-04-14](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscalers-control-most-compute)); on the
owner table's own denominator the programme's step 1 counts about 69 % (`programme/STATE.md`). On 2026-11-11 the
US Commerce Department was reported to be finishing a rule that would require licences for Blackwell-class chips in
seven more countries and exempt US-run data centres that keep embargoed countries' users out; no text had been
published by 2026-11-13 ([Asia Times, relaying The Information](https://asiatimes.com/2026/11/huangs-fall-us-curbs-put-chinas-ai-computing-in-peril)).
On 2026-11-13 two senators asked Commerce to condition chip licences on barring Chinese firms' remote access
([Senator Blunt Rochester](https://www.bluntrochester.senate.gov/news/press-releases/news-senators-blunt-rochester-curtis-demand-trump-administration-strengthen-ai-chip-export-controls/)).
The subject of cycles 013 and 014. Data: Epoch AI's chip owners dataset. Attention: **loud** (7 signals, 1 converged day).

**9. Ebola in Congo, still running** (health). On data to 2026-11-11 Congo's Bundibugyo outbreak stood at 11,197
confirmed cases, including 5,471 deaths, in eight provinces, with community transmission ongoing; Kenya had nine
confirmed cases and two deaths, the last case reported on 2026-10-20 ([ECDC](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/ebola-disease-multi-country-outbreak-2026);
read again on 2026-11-16, no newer count from ECDC). WHO said on 2026-11-13 that 400 to 500 cases are still identified every
week ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168797)). The subject of cycle 012. Data: INSP SitReps, HDX.
Attention: **quiet** (58 signals).

**10. COP31 in its last week, on a 2.8-degree path** (climate). COP31 opened in Antalya on 2026-11-09 and is set to
close on 2026-11-20. The subsidiary bodies closed in the night of 14 to 15 November and forwarded adaptation, finance and mitigation
texts unresolved; the presidency set four tracks for the second week, two of them ministerial, and a first draft of
the cover decision came out on 2026-11-16, while several groups oppose a cover decision ([UNFCCC, unedited draft](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/dt_cop31_cover_decision.pdf);
[Earth Negotiations Bulletin, 2026-11-14](https://enb.iisd.org/antalya-un-climate-change-conference-cop31-daily-report-14nov2026);
[report: Third World Network, 2026-11-16](https://twn.my/title2/climate/info.service/2026/cc261109.htm)). UNEP finds that current policies could take warming to 2.8 °C this century
([UNEP, 2026-10-21](https://www.unep.org/en/news-and-stories/press-release/new-unep-report-lays-out-emission-cuts-needed-return-15degc));
WMO puts January to September 2026 at about 1.47 °C above pre-industrial ([WMO, 2026-11-09](https://wmo.int/resources/publication-series/state-of-climate-update-cop/state-of-climate-update-cop31)).
Open question: the gap between pledges and policies, country by country. Data: UNEP EGR data, Climate Watch.
Attention: **quiet** (71 signals).

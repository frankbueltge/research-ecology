## The matters

**1. Ebola in Congo, still running.** On data to 2026-11-03, Congo's Bundibugyo outbreak stood at 10,668
confirmed cases, including 5,198 deaths, in eight provinces, with community transmission ongoing; a week
earlier the last briefing had 10,274 cases on 2026-10-29. Kenya has nine confirmed cases and two deaths, its
last case reported on 2026-10-20; Nigeria's one case, reported 2026-10-20, was evacuated to Switzerland
([ECDC, updated 2026-11-05](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/ebola-disease-multi-country-outbreak-2026); daily
reports: [INSP](https://insp.cd/ebola-17eme-epidemie/)). Urgent after: WHO's emergency of international
concern (ECDC above). Open question: the subject of cycle 012, which a proposal may not repeat. Data: INSP
SitReps, OCHA HDX. Attention: **quiet** (13 signals, 0 converged days). Line: withheld — Q11, Q14.

**2. Interest before hospitals.** UNCTAD counts 3.7 billion people in 51 developing countries that spend more
on interest than on health or on education, 1.2 billion more than in 2010; developing countries' net
interest on public debt reached $995 billion in 2025, and in Africa the share of people living in such
countries rose from 24 % in 2010 to 65 % in 2023 ([UNCTAD, A World of Debt 2026](https://unctad.org/publication/world-of-debt)).
Urgent after: UNCTAD ("the futures of a growing share of the developing world's population at risk").
Open question, as UNCTAD frames it: what borrowing at developed countries' rates would free, about $500
billion a year. Data: UNCTAD's World of Debt dashboard, World Bank International Debt Statistics, WHO Global
Health Expenditure Database, UNESCO UIS. Attention: **absent** (0 signals).

**3. Appeals less than half funded.** On 2026-10-28 the 2026 Global Humanitarian Overview had received
US$15.78 billion of US$35.06 billion, 45.0 %; Niger's plan was 19.8 % funded, Burkina Faso's 24.4 %, and the
United States gave 31.7 % of the tracked money ([OCHA FTS](https://fts.unocha.org/plans/overview/2026)).
UNHCR counted 117.8 million people forcibly displaced at end-2025 ([UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/about-unhcr/overview/figures-glance)).
Urgent after: OCHA's tracked coverage. Open question: which plans are cut deepest, and what the cut leaves
undone. Data: OCHA FTS API, HDX. Attention: **absent** (3 signals). Line: withheld — Q8.

**4. The air that kills millions.** The State of Global Air 2025 attributes 7.9 million deaths in 2023 to
air pollution, 4.9 million to ambient PM2.5, 2.8 million to household air pollution and 470,000 to ozone;
India and China each had more than two million ([HEI, 2025-10-22](https://www.stateofglobalair.org/news-events/2025/new-state-global-air-2025-report-shows-nearly-nine-ten-global-air-pollution-deaths);
[summary](https://www.stateofglobalair.org/sites/default/files/documents/2025-10/soga-2025-annual-report-executive-summary-infographic.pdf)).
These are model estimates, revised with each edition. Urgent after: HEI, "the leading environmental risk
factor for death". Open question: where exposure is known only from models because no monitor stands.
Data: State of Global Air data tool, OpenAQ, WHO air quality database. Attention: **absent** (7 signals).

**5. One in ten below three dollars a day.** The World Bank counts 824.3 million people, 10.1 %, below
$3.00 a day (2021 PPP) in 2024, revised down from 846.8 million in its March vintage; its nowcast falls
only to 9.8 % in 2026, and extreme poverty is rising in the Middle East, North Africa, Afghanistan and
Pakistan region and remains high in Sub-Saharan Africa ([World Bank, September 2026 update](https://blogs.worldbank.org/en/opendata/september-2026-global-poverty-update-from-the-world-bank--one-in)).
Urgent after: the Bank's own reading of a stalled decline. Open question: how much a revision of the same
year moves the count, and for whom. Data: PIP API. Attention: **absent** (1 signal).

**6. Three quarters of the world's wealth.** By the World Inequality Lab's estimates for 2025 the global
top 10 % held 75 % of wealth and the bottom half 2 %; the top 0.001 % held nearly 6.1 %, up from around 3.8 %
in 1995 ([WIR 2026, executive summary](https://wir2026.wid.world/insight/executive-summary/)). Urgent after:
WEF's Global Risks Report, which names inequality the most interconnected risk (MAP.md). Open question:
how wealth at the top is measured where tax records are closed. Data: World Inequality Database.
Attention: **absent** (6 signals).

**7. Five companies, most of the machines.** Epoch AI estimates that Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft and
Oracle held 71 % of the world's cumulative AI compute in Q4 2025, up from 63 % in Q1 2024, in
H100-equivalents, on incomplete records of who owns chips ([Epoch AI, 2026-04-14](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscalers-control-most-compute)).
Urgent after: Epoch AI's series. Open question: who owns the rest, and what the estimate cannot see.
Data: Epoch AI's AI chip owners dataset (CSV). Attention: **absent** (9 signals).

**8. Three in four people under autocracy.** V-Dem's best estimate is 92 autocracies and 87 democracies at
end-2025, with 74 % of people living in autocracies, up from 50 % in 2005 ([V-Dem 2026](https://www.v-dem.net/documents/75/V-Dem_Institute_Democracy_Report_2026_lowres.pdf));
Freedom House recorded a 20th consecutive year of decline, 54 countries down and 35 up ([Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/article/new-report-global-freedom-declined-20th-consecutive-year-2025)).
Urgent after: both indices. Open question: where the two disagree on one country, and why. Data: V-Dem
dataset, Freedom House scores. Attention: **quiet** (11 signals).

**9. The warmest October, with El Niño rising.** October 2026 was the warmest October in ERA5, 1.75 °C above
1850–1900 and the third month in a row above 1.5 °C; the twelve months to it averaged 1.51 °C, extra-polar
sea surface temperature set an October record, and an exceptionally strong El Niño kept strengthening
([Copernicus](https://climate.copernicus.eu/surface-air-temperature-october-2026)). Urgent after:
Copernicus, which expects 2026 to exceed 1.5 °C. Open question: which harvests and cities the event hits
first. Data: ERA5, NOAA ENSO indices, CHIRPS. Attention: **quiet** (16 signals).

**10. COP31 opens on a 2.8-degree path.** UNEP's Emissions Gap Report (2026-10-21) finds that current
policies could take warming to 2.8 °C this century and full NDCs to 2.6 °C, worse than last year's
projections after the US withdrawal from the Paris Agreement and revised historical emissions
([UNEP press release](https://www.unep.org/en/news-and-stories/press-release/new-unep-report-lays-out-emission-cuts-needed-return-15degc);
[report](https://wedocs.unep.org/items/87c02cac-8da8-4c28-ba61-c846ee68ea70)). COP31 runs in Antalya from
9 to 20 November ([report: Tantalya guide](https://tantalya.com/cop31/guide)). Open question: the gap
between pledges and policies, country by country. Data: UNEP EGR data, Climate Watch NDCs.
Attention: **absent** (5 signals); it is likely to grow once the summit opens.

## The matters

**1. Who checks the tests of frontier AI** (ai). On 2026-11-18 the Commons committee on business, innovation,
science and trade found that AI developers "apply different safety thresholds and remain responsible for judging
whether their own systems meet them", and asked for a statutory regulator, separate from the AI Security Institute,
with powers to require independent verification of safety claims ([UK Parliament](https://committees.parliament.uk/committee/365/business-innovation-science-and-trade-committee/news/219006/close-ai-safety-gaps-to-secure-britains-400-billion-ai-growth-opportunity-mps-urge/);
[report](https://committees.parliament.uk/publications/55827/standard/pdf)). On 2026-11-16 California's Government
Operations Agency recommended licensed independent verifiers with full access to frontier developers ([GovOps](https://govops.ca.gov/wp-content/uploads/sites/11/2026/11/FINAL_-AI-Safety-in-2026-and-Beyond-v2.pdf)),
and the European Commission's chief scientific advisers asked Europe to build its own capacity to evaluate AI models ([European Commission](https://research-and-innovation.ec.europa.eu/news/all-research-and-innovation-news/ai-requires-rigorous-safeguards-and-rules-keep-pace-technological-advances-experts-tell-european-2026-11-16_en)).
None of the three is law. The subject of cycle 016 (`programme/STATE.md`, step 4). Data: the International AI Safety
Report 2026. Attention: **quiet** (24 signals).

**2. Interest before hospitals** (economy). UNCTAD counts 3.7 billion people in 51 developing countries that spend
more on interest than on health or on education, 1.2 billion more than in 2010; developing countries paid $995
billion in net interest on public debt in 2025 ([UNCTAD, A World of Debt 2026](https://unctad.org/publication/world-of-debt)).
Open question, as UNCTAD frames it: what borrowing at developed countries' rates would free. Data: UNCTAD's
dashboard, World Bank International Debt Statistics, WHO Global Health Expenditure Database. Attention: **absent** (1 signal).

**3. Appeals less than half funded** (displacement). On 2026-11-19 the 2026 Global Humanitarian Overview had
received US$15.81 billion of US$35.06 billion, 45.1 %; Niger's plan stood at 20.0 %, Yemen's at 23.0 % and
Afghanistan's at 33.9 %, and the United States gave 30.8 % of the money tracked ([OCHA FTS](https://fts.unocha.org/plans/overview/2026)).
UNHCR counted one in every 70 people on Earth forcibly displaced at end-June 2026 ([UNHCR, Mid-Year Trends 2026](https://www.unhcr.org/us/mid-year-trends)).
Open question: which plans are cut deepest and what the cut leaves undone. Data: OCHA FTS API, HDX. Attention: **quiet** (13 signals).

**4. Monitored wildlife, still in decline** (nature). WWF's Living Planet Report 2026 finds that monitored wildlife
populations declined by 73 % on average between 1970 and 2022, across 35,803 populations of 5,790 vertebrate species;
freshwater populations by 85 %, and by region Latin America and the Caribbean by 95 %, while half of the populations
tracked are stable or increasing ([WWF, 2026-10-07](https://www.worldwildlife.org/news/press-releases/wildlife-population-sizes-decline-73-on-average-but-tiger-elephant-turtle-and-tuna-recoveries-offer-hope/);
[report page](https://www.worldwildlife.org/publications/2026-living-planet-report/)); the index measures the average
change in population size, not animals lost. The IUCN Red List's next update is set for 2026-12-03 ([IUCN](https://www.iucnredlist.org/assessment/updates)).
Open question: what the average hides between populations that recover and those that collapse. Data: Living Planet
Index database, IUCN Red List API. Attention: **quiet** (19 signals).

**5. Three quarters of the world's wealth** (inequality). By the World Inequality Lab's estimates for 2025 the global
top 10 % held 75 % of wealth and the bottom half 2 %; the top 0.001 % held nearly 6.1 %, up from around 3.8 % in 1995
([WIR 2026, executive summary](https://wir2026.wid.world/insight/executive-summary/)). Urgent after: WEF's Global
Risks Report 2026, which names inequality the most interconnected risk for a second year ([WEF](https://www.weforum.org/publications/global-risks-report-2026)).
Open question: how wealth at the top is measured where tax records are closed. Data: World Inequality Database.
Attention: **quiet** (10 signals).

**6. A fifth of those counted, in crisis; Congo at a record** (hunger). 266 million people in 47 food-crisis countries
and territories faced high acute food insecurity in 2025, 22.9 % of those analysed ([GRFC mid-year update 2026](https://www.fightfoodcrises.net/articles/mid-year-update-2026-grfc-now-available)).
The IPC analysis for DR Congo, announced on 2026-11-13, projects a record 30.3 million people in Crisis or worse from January
to June 2027; nearly 29 million face it from September to December 2026, 3.6 million of them in Emergency
([WHO, FAO, WFP, UNICEF](https://www.who.int/news/item/13-11-2026-conflict-and-disease-outbreaks-deepen-dr-congo-s-hunger-crisis-as-record-numbers-are-projected-to-face-acute-food-insecurity)).
Open question: what the IPC analysis cannot see where access is denied. Data: IPC API, FEWS NET. Attention: **loud** (40 signals, 1 converged day).

**7. Three in four people under autocracy** (democracy). V-Dem's best estimate is 92 autocracies and 87 democracies
at end-2025, with 74 % of people, 6 billion, living in autocracies, up from 50 % in 2005 ([V-Dem 2026](https://www.v-dem.net/documents/75/V-Dem_Institute_Democracy_Report_2026_lowres.pdf));
Freedom House recorded a 20th consecutive year of decline, 54 countries down and 35 up ([Freedom House](https://freedomhouse.org/article/new-report-global-freedom-declined-20th-consecutive-year-2025)).
Urgent after: both indices. Open question: where the two disagree on one country, and why. Data: V-Dem dataset,
Freedom House scores. Attention: **quiet** (10 signals).

**8. A record of conflicts between states** (war). UCDP counted about 244,600 people killed in organised violence in
2025, up from about 187,000 in 2024, and 65 state-based conflicts, the most in its records, eight of them between
states ([Uppsala University, 2026-06-09](https://www.uu.se/en/press/press-releases/2026/2026-06-09-ucdp-record-number-of-conflicts-between-states);
[Journal of Peace Research](https://academic.oup.com/jpr/article/63/4/705/8703754)). SIPRI put world military spending
at $2,887 billion in 2025 ([SIPRI](https://www.sipri.org/publications/2026/sipri-fact-sheets/trends-world-military-expenditure-2025)).
Open question: why conflicts between states and killings of civilians rise while non-state conflicts fall. Data:
UCDP datasets, SIPRI Milex. Attention: **loud** (52 signals, 2 converged days).

**9. Five companies, most of the machines** (power). Epoch AI estimates that Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft and
Oracle held 71 % of the world's cumulative AI compute in Q4 2025, in H100-equivalents, on incomplete records of who
owns chips ([Epoch AI, 2026-04-14](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscalers-control-most-compute)). By a US rule issued
on 2026-11-16 and in force from 2026-11-19, a data centre anywhere needs a licence for chips with which it would give
more than 12,000,000 "aggregate TPP" to firms headquartered in China, Macau or another D:5 country, and advanced chips
for Indonesia, Malaysia, Singapore, Thailand and Brazil need one too, except for US-headquartered firms
([BIS](https://www.bis.gov/press-release/bis-issues-rule-addressing-remote-access-american-super-intelligence-technology);
[Federal Register](https://public-inspection.federalregister.gov/2026-23529.pdf)); its ground is national security.
The subject of cycles 013 and 014. Data: Epoch AI's chip owners dataset. Attention: **loud** (8 signals, 1 converged day).

**10. Ebola in Congo, still running** (health). On data to 2026-11-15 Congo's Bundibugyo outbreak stood at 11,432
confirmed cases, including 5,593 deaths; 442 cases were confirmed in that week against 458 the week before, and
Kenya's last patient left care on 2026-11-11 ([WHO AFRO, situation report 27](https://afro.who.int/countries/kenya/publication/ebola-bundibugyo-virus-disease-outbreak-democratic-republic-congo-uganda-weekly-external-situation)).
On 2026-11-17 WHO's Director-General said two thirds of confirmed deaths occur outside treatment centres, and children
account for over a quarter of confirmed cases and a third of deaths ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168827)). Cycle 012's subject. Data: INSP SitReps, HDX. Attention: **quiet** (84 signals).

**11. COP31 in its last days, on a 2.8-degree path** (climate). COP31 opened in Antalya on 2026-11-09 and is set to
close on 2026-11-20; a first draft of the cover decision came out on 2026-11-16 ([UNFCCC, unedited draft](https://unfccc.int/sites/default/files/resource/dt_cop31_cover_decision.pdf)).
On 18 November divisions persisted over mitigation, carbon-market guidance and finance for adaptation and loss and
damage, while a decision on the least developed countries was agreed ([Earth Negotiations Bulletin, 2026-11-19](https://enb.iisd.org/sites/default/files/2026-11/enb12910e.pdf)).
UNEP finds that current policies could take warming to 2.8 °C this century ([UNEP, 2026-10-21](https://www.unep.org/en/news-and-stories/press-release/new-unep-report-lays-out-emission-cuts-needed-return-15degc));
WMO puts January to September 2026 at about 1.47 °C above pre-industrial ([WMO, 2026-11-09](https://wmo.int/resources/publication-series/state-of-climate-update-cop/state-of-climate-update-cop31)).
Open question: the gap between pledges and policies, country by country. Data: UNEP EGR data, Climate Watch.
Attention: **loud** (100 signals, 1 converged day).

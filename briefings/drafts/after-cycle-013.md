## The matters

**1. Five companies, most of the machines** (power). Epoch AI estimates that Amazon, Google, Meta, Microsoft and
Oracle held 71 % of the world's cumulative AI compute in Q4 2025, up from 63 % in Q1 2024, in H100-equivalents, on
incomplete records of who owns chips ([Epoch AI, 2026-04-14](https://epoch.ai/data-insights/hyperscalers-control-most-compute)).
The subject of cycle 013, step 1 of the programme: what the ecology found stands in `programme/STATE.md`.
Data: Epoch AI's chip owners dataset. Attention: **absent** (6 signals).

**2. Interest before hospitals** (economy). UNCTAD counts 3.7 billion people in 51 developing countries that spend
more on interest than on health or on education, 1.2 billion more than in 2010; developing countries paid $995
billion in net interest on public debt in 2025, and in Africa the share of people in such countries rose from 24 %
in 2010 to 65 % in 2023 ([UNCTAD, A World of Debt 2026](https://unctad.org/publication/world-of-debt)). Urgent after:
UNCTAD. Open question, as UNCTAD frames it: what borrowing at developed countries' rates would free. Data: UNCTAD's
dashboard, World Bank International Debt Statistics, WHO Global Health Expenditure Database. Attention: **absent** (0).

**3. Appeals less than half funded** (displacement). On 2026-10-28 the 2026 Global Humanitarian Overview had
received US$15.78 billion of US$35.06 billion, 45.0 % ([OCHA FTS](https://fts.unocha.org/plans/overview/2026)); on
OCHA's plan table of 2026-11-08 Sudan's plan stood at 48.7 % funded, Somalia's at 35.6 % and Afghanistan's at 32.7 %
([OCHA Humanitarian Action](https://humanitarianaction.info/)). UNHCR counted 117.8 million people forcibly displaced
at end-2025 ([UNHCR](https://www.unhcr.org/about-unhcr/overview/figures-glance)). Open question: which plans are cut
deepest and what the cut leaves undone. Data: OCHA FTS API, HDX. Attention: **absent** (4 signals).

**4. The air that kills millions** (nature). The State of Global Air 2025 attributes 7.9 million deaths in 2023 to
air pollution, 4.9 million to ambient PM2.5 and 2.8 million to household air pollution; India and China each had
more than two million ([HEI, 2025-10-22](https://www.stateofglobalair.org/news-events/2025/new-state-global-air-2025-report-shows-nearly-nine-ten-global-air-pollution-deaths)).
Model estimates, revised with each edition. Open question: where exposure is known only from models. Data: State of
Global Air data tool, OpenAQ. Attention: **loud** (9 signals, 1 converged day).

**5. A record of wars between states** (war). UCDP counted about 244,600 people killed in organised violence in 2025,
up from about 187,000 in 2024, and 65 state-based conflicts, the most in its records
([Uppsala University, 2026-06-09](https://www.uu.se/en/press/press-releases/2026/2026-06-09-ucdp-record-number-of-conflicts-between-states);
[Journal of Peace Research](https://academic.oup.com/jpr/article/63/4/705/8703754)). SIPRI put world military spending
at $2,887 billion in 2025, up 2.9 % in real terms ([SIPRI](https://www.sipri.org/publications/2026/sipri-fact-sheets/trends-world-military-expenditure-2025)).
Open question: why conflicts between states multiply while UCDP's other two types move differently. Data: UCDP
datasets, SIPRI Milex. Attention: **loud** (44 signals, 2 converged days).

**6. A fifth of those counted, in crisis** (hunger). 266 million people in 47 food-crisis countries and territories
faced high acute food insecurity in 2025, 22.9 % of those analysed ([GRFC mid-year update 2026](https://www.fightfoodcrises.net/articles/mid-year-update-2026-grfc-now-available));
SOFI 2026 nowcasts 645 million undernourished in 2025 ([FAO](https://openknowledge.fao.org/3/cd8306en/cd8306en.pdf)).
Coverage changes every year, so the share, not the total, compares. Open question: what the IPC analysis cannot see
where access is denied. Data: IPC API, FEWS NET. Attention: **quiet** (11 signals).

**7. Press freedom at a 25-year low** (information). RSF's 2026 index rates press freedom "difficult" or "very
serious" in 52.2 % of 180 countries and territories, against 13.7 % in 2002 ([RSF](https://rsf.org/en/2026-rsf-index-press-freedom-25-year-low));
trust in news averaged 37 % across 48 markets, the lowest since 2015 ([Reuters Institute](https://reutersinstitute.politics.ox.ac.uk/digital-news-report/2026/dnr-executive-summary)).
Open question: how far the RSF questionnaire and the tally of abuses agree country by country. Data: RSF index
scores, CPJ databases. Attention: **absent** (2 signals).

**8. More AI harms on record** (ai). The AI Incident Database recorded 362 incidents in 2025, up from 233 in 2024
([Stanford HAI, AI Index 2026](https://hai.stanford.edu/ai-index/2026-ai-index-report)), a count of what was reported,
not of how often harm occurred. Employment at ages 22–25 in highly AI-exposed occupations was about 19 % below
less-exposed peers by June 2026, descriptive, not causal ([Stanford Digital Economy Lab](https://digitaleconomy.stanford.edu/news/canariesaug26/)).
Step 3 of the programme, in the draft revision of its plan, reads this record. Data: AIID snapshots. Attention: **loud** (52 signals, 2 converged days).

**9. Ebola in Congo, still running** (health). On data to 2026-11-04 Congo's Bundibugyo outbreak stood at 10,741
confirmed cases, including 5,230 deaths, in eight provinces, with community transmission ongoing; Kenya had nine
confirmed cases and two deaths as of 2026-10-26, and Nigeria's one case was evacuated to Switzerland
([ECDC, updated 2026-11-05](https://www.ecdc.europa.eu/en/ebola-disease-multi-country-outbreak-2026)). The subject of
cycle 012. Data: INSP SitReps, HDX. Attention: **quiet** (17 signals).

**10. COP31 opens on a 2.8-degree path** (climate). COP31 opened in Antalya on 2026-11-09 and is set to close on
2026-11-20 ([UN News](https://news.un.org/en/story/2026/11/1168763)). UNEP finds that current policies could take
warming to 2.8 °C this century and full NDCs to 2.6 °C ([UNEP, 2026-10-21](https://www.unep.org/en/news-and-stories/press-release/new-unep-report-lays-out-emission-cuts-needed-return-15degc)).
October 2026 was the warmest October in ERA5 ([Copernicus](https://climate.copernicus.eu/surface-air-temperature-october-2026)).
Open question: the gap between pledges and policies, country by country. Data: UNEP EGR data, Climate Watch.
Attention: **quiet** (18 signals).
